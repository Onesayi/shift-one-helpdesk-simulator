# GradeTile

The 96px letter grade at the top of the shift report, a white letter on a fixed grade colour, beside the score out of 100.

- Markup: `<div class="report-head"><div class="grade g-A">A</div><div class="big-score">91<small>/100</small></div></div>`.
- Colours: `grade-a` … `grade-f` (no E). They are fixed and do not change with the theme.
